Send videos from any webpage

Browsers don't let one site read another site's pages, so for most webpages the player can't look inside by itself. This bookmarklet runs on the page you're watching, finds its video and opens it here.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it: ▶ Send to Frame
  3. On any page with a video, click the bookmark.

Works for normal video files, HLS streams (.m3u8) and embedded YouTube videos. It can't play paid streaming sites (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar…), and some sites block their videos from playing anywhere else.
